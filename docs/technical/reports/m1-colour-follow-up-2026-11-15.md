# M1: all-colour Match follow-up, November 15, 2026

## Finding for Steve

**The shift is not fixed across colours.** The previous fix removes premature
channel clipping, especially the visible denim error around 0 stops. Bright
green/cyan/indigo and other colours still miss Match's own target, sometimes
more than before. This is not all an unavoidable HDR-to-SDR difference. The
remaining recipe/ordering problem needs a decision about highlight colour,
brightness and editability, and Film Look placement. No further pixel change,
gate change, tolerance change, migration or hidden correction is implemented
in this follow-up. M1 stays open; M1a is a scoped clipping fix, not colour
equivalence for the entire photograph.

## Stage attribution

1. **Target compression is inherent, but does not explain residual errors.**
   `build_sdr_match_target` applies its 90%-white shoulder and then the existing
   sRGB gamut mapper. A coloured highlight cannot retain HDR brightness and
   saturation inside SDR white. Target and HDR arrays are identical between
   these two versions for these grades. Example: green at +2 stops / 75%
   saturation has inherent Delta E00 48.069, but still has chroma. HEAD adds
   **23.189 residual Delta E00** and takes its normalized chroma almost to zero.
   The nominal -117.9-degree residual hue there is undefined, not evidence of
   a coherent green-to-other-hue rotation. [Implementation](../../../codebase/backend/hdr_finisher/sdr_match.py).
2. **The remaining bright errors are largely avoidable target-reproduction
   errors, not a new defect in the final mapper.** Generated SDR still applies
   an early shoulder and gamut map *before* Exposure Bands and contrast. The
   fitter can then lift already-compressed colours above white again. Final
   legal-gamut mapping removes chroma from these raised values. For Film-off
   green +2 / 75%, early gamut changes Delta E00 by 9.358; Exposure Bands adds
   +14.048 L*, contrast +3.670 L*, and final gamut changes Delta E00 by 25.415.
   These are stage-to-stage changes, **not additive error contributions**.
   Curves and wheels are identities here. The final mapper is behaving as
   specified; shader parity alone cannot repair a bad fitted input.
   [Order](../../../codebase/backend/hdr_finisher/adjustments.py),
   [mapper](../../../codebase/backend/hdr_finisher/sdr_gamut.py).
3. **Acceptance does not mean highlight-colour equivalence.** Body metrics
   use captured HDR <=0.9. Highlight refinement minimizes mean finished
   highlight **luminance**, subject to body gates and no increase in white
   area. It does not impose a per-family or highlight-colour gate, or check
   future +0.5 EV edits. No new limits were invented here.
   [Fitter](../../../codebase/backend/hdr_finisher/sdr_match.py).
4. **The old -1-stop 2..3 Delta E00 is not inherent highlight compression.**
   The quoted full-population Film-off result is reproduced exactly. At 75%
   saturation baseline chroma ratios are 0.913..0.943 and Delta L about -2.03
   to -2.08 across the seven hues. HEAD ratios are 1.000, essentially zero
   hue shift, and Delta L -0.964..-1.061 (Delta E00 0.914..1.007). Remaining
   error is chiefly the neutral/image fit's slight darkness, not a blue-only
   rotation or final gamut clipping. With sRGB-only support, the maximum over
   saturations is only 0.499 Delta E00; with Film and wide controls it remains
   3.26..3.90. Do not call the two populations the same experiment.
5. **M1d is still real.** In the common Film-exposure space, median SDR/HDR
   input Y ratios at -3/-1/0/+1 stops (sRGB-only population) are Portra
   1.159/1.064/0.960/0.784, Ektar 1.171/1.076/0.959/0.776, Velvia
   1.017/1.012/0.987/0.677. Stocks respond differently to those inputs.
   Direct equal-input stock-kernel controls agree <=0.321 Delta E00 across
   all eight stops and <=0.0071 through 0 stops; this is a kernel control,
   not proof that the full Film chains at different placements are equivalent.
   Moving Film Look or inserting a Match-only pre-look correction is a design
   change, not an authorised incidental bug fix.

## A7R00619 +0.5 EV stress case

Film off, same reduced RAW source and fresh Match. Baseline white area is
9.876% ->15.294% (+5.418 points); HEAD is 15.432% ->27.026% (+11.594 points).
HEAD's pre-Bands gamut output is only 0.437% / 0.542% white (untrimmed/trimmed).
Exposure Bands raises that to 12.516% / 22.375%; contrast to 15.363% / 26.965%;
final gamut finishes at 15.432% / 27.026%. Thus the trim problem is created
principally **after** the early shoulder, not by lack of an early peak cap.
At HEAD, 12.482% / 22.310% of pixels exceed linear white after Bands.

## Choices for Steve (not implemented)

1. **First decision: should Match explicitly fit the complete finished colour
   target, including highlights, before accepting a recipe?** Recommended
   direction: search ordinary controls against body **and** bright colour,
   preserving existing gates; retain a conservative result or report review
   when there is no good ordinary recipe. Current worst Film-off bright patch
   is 25.131 Delta E00; fixing only blue at 0 stops is insufficient. This
   needs a colour-versus-brightness/editability specification; no new local
   tolerance or weighting is approved here. More fitting passes may cost time.
2. After that decision, choose whether SDR editing should protect highlight
   colour at the cost of brightness. A7R00619 Detail 35 illustrates the price
   above. A broad shoulder/order redesign affects ordinary SDR grades, not just
   Match, and must respect T1-a (tone -> look -> final cap). It is not a reason
   to bypass the existing calibrated-scene guard.
3. M1d: keep current Film placement and accept/report its exposure-dependent
   difference, or design fitting that preserves the captured Film input. The
   +1-stop median input shortfall is 22% Portra, 22% Ektar, 32% Velvia. Moving
   the look or adding a hidden Match renderer is not implemented.

Simply deferring the **early** gamut boundary is not a proven general repair:
on the fixed sRGB-only recipes, Film-off +2 maximum residual falls
24.452 ->16.887, but Portra +1 rises 12.815 ->17.388. It still loses bright
colour and changes what Film sees. This numerical ablation is not a refit
or an export-parity validation and cannot justify changing the pipeline.
