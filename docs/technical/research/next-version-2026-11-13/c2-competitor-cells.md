HDR Finisher's clearest confirmed delivery advantage over Capture One and PhotoLab is AVIF/JPEG XL output, while Lightroom already supplies JPEG gain maps and true HDR editing with SDR rendition controls ([Adobe HDR guide][AH]). Full independent HDR and SDR grades remain a useful concrete positioning claim, but this research does not prove an exclusive all-three lead: Capture One/PhotoLab rows 42–46, 51–52 and 54 remain U, as do Lightroom's AVIF gain-map, JPEG XL fallback and headroom Proof details. The Adobe guide still explicitly excludes Library Grid from HDR display. Library parity is stronger than the earlier matrix showed; HDR Finisher also trails the documented AI selection/portrait and RAW-denoise workflows. Adobe's existing H cells for masks, depth masks, Generative Remove and Denoise were rechecked in its [masking][AM] and [Enhance][AD] guides/navigation and remain unchanged; Capture One generative removal remains U.
