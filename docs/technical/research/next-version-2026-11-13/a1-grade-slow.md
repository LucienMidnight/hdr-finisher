## 1. HSL Show selection

**Cause — measured:** confirmed from W11: SDR Fit 1.470–1.961 s, native 1.452–1.661 s, versus HDR Fit 0.390–0.756 s; hiding 19–33 ms. Sampling takes 1.426–1.729 s; the slow cold example also has approximately 230 ms outside the request (`performance-table.md:573-596`). The retained CPU profile records 1.511 s total, 1.144 s grade rebuild, 0.699 s highlight compression and 0.404 s gamut compression (`w11-profile.log`, final SDR profile). Approximately 0.33–0.37 s remains in preparation, selection and response work; preparation alone is 0.196 s in that profile. These are not independent extra costs on top of total.

**Cause — read in code:** `backend/hdr_finisher/hsl_sampling.py:56-109` obtains a source proxy, calls `apply_adjustments` to tap the requested HSL input, prepares/selects coverage, creates RGBA and PNG/base64. This route bypasses the existing `_hue_stages` cache (`render_cache.py:1114-1139`). That cache also keys every adjustment, too broadly for reuse across downstream changes. Local selections have different taps and mask multiplication; global and local caches cannot be conflated.

**Proposed fix — estimated:** cheapest first implementation is a bounded CPU global stage/preparation entry in `hsl_sampling.py` and `render_cache.py`, reused across show/hide, range changes and downstream edits. Key source epoch, lane, proxy edge, colour context, geometry and all pre-HSL settings (including captured Match dependencies), excluding visibility, range recipe and post-HSL controls. Retain the prepared selection input as well as stage pixels only where necessary; preserve local route initially. This removes the repeated 1.14 s rebuild on a hit. First activation after upstream edits still misses; do not describe this as a universal activation fix.

For first activation, preferred second step is a preview-only GPU coverage overlay in `frontend/webgpu-preview.js`, `hsl-panel.js` and the HSL shader. Reuse the exact stage/selection computation, draw cyan coverage to a separate overlay surface, avoid image-sized readback and PNG. Keep CPU sampling fallback and CPU colour picking. This has credible >50% whole-wait potential, unlike accelerating PNG alone. Reject caching only the final overlay: it misses on range edits and does not remove cold upstream work. Reject changing SDR compression to approximate the stage: it changes selection meaning.

**Expected whole-wait gain — estimated:** warm CPU hits approximately 1.5 s to 0.35–0.6 s (60–75%); cold unchanged. GPU stage reuse plausibly 0.05–0.25 s warm supported preview, but not measured and dependent on exact stage availability. Effort 4–8 agent-hours CPU cache; 12–20 hours GPU overlay and fallback/parity.

**Memory/VRAM — estimated:** at the request's maximum 1600 edge (`hsl_sampling.py:23`), 3:2 image has 1.71 MP: float32 RGB 20.5 MB plus RGBA overlay 6.8 MB; square upper bounds 30.7/10.2 MB. Prepared selection planes are additional: count their actual unique backing before admission. Fit here is bounded sampling resolution, not display dimensions. One lane/entry, evict within the existing 600 MB owner; no full-native stage. GPU alternative adds coverage at 2 bytes/pixel for r16float (3.4 MB at 3:2 1600), or 4 for RGBA8, plus any stage that is not already live; account in adapter budget. Do not retain a second whole photograph merely to show an overlay.

**Risk:** stale or wrong-lane coverage, local mask mismatch, stage tap differing from real render; preview-only design avoids changing export/Proof. Shared selection shader changes invoke Still strict and existing HSL delivery checks. **Test plan:** fast suites when implemented; `hsl-selection-engine.js` and `hsl-overlay-review.js`, serial locked, plus `hsl-selection-delivery.js` if shared shader changes. Verify upstream invalidation, downstream reuse, local fallback, show/hide photograph restoration, cancellation and cache pressure. **Proposed tolerance:** whole wait <1 s in both views, repeated timing differences <=10% treated as noise; coverage must meet existing selection checks, settled picture unchanged. **Confidence:** high on cause/warm CPU benefit; medium on cold GPU estimate.

## 2. SDR Match entire

**Cause/where time goes — measured:** W11 has warm request 1.012–1.123 s, cold 1.300 s including 234 ms source proxy; 11 GPU candidates, nine exposure candidates. Exposure refinement 489–551 ms, neutral fit 164–215 ms, CPU certification 131–137 ms; GPU candidate waiting 226–283 ms is included in these stages. Final picture adds 60–140 ms. Remaining roughly 0.2–0.3 s covers target/scoring/merge, not scheduler dead time (`performance-table.md:598-609`; main output `w11-match-before.json`).

**Read in code:** search is already coarse-to-fine: baseline, six offsets ±0.10/0.20/0.30 EV, then ±0.05 about the winner (`sdr_match.py:165-212`). It optimizes image P95 luminance while constraining colour regression to 0.003. Exposure is inside nonlinear tone/mapping, so a linear exposure formula is an initializer, not a substitute for candidate rendering. Sequential acceptance also means pruning can change the selected recipe.

**Tolerances — read in code:** `docs/technical/sdr-match.md` describes snapshot/undo semantics but supplies no numeric fit thresholds. Actual thresholds are `sdr_match.py:43,464-485`: reject nonfinite, P95 luma >0.06 or OKLab >0.05; normal requires median luma <=0.01, P95 <=0.03, median OKLab <=0.015, P95 <=0.04 and safe highlight ordering. Intermediate refinement guards at lines 109-123 also apply. These are target-fit gates, not permission to change finished output by 5%. General finished-file tolerances remain mean <1 / p99.9 <=3 in perceptual units (`grade.md:1701-1704`), with exact HDR target cap. Do not loosen either kind.

**Proposed fix — estimated:** first reuse invariant target/body statistics, colour transforms and candidate input preparation in `sdr_match.py` / candidate bridge, retaining the same candidate order and CPU certification. Then trial an analytic log-luma exposure suggestion on the body mask, snapped to the existing grid: test suggestion and adjacent points, fall back to the full search if quality or bracketing is uncertain. Keep original search as reference during validation; final accepted result must preserve quality/status and finished-pixel tolerances. This is an experimental option, not proven equivalent. Reject unconditional three-point/binary search: no demonstrated unimodality of the constrained P95 objective. Reject GPU-only certification: risks export mismatch to save only about 0.13 s.

**Expected whole-wait gain — estimated:** eliminating four of nine exposure renders saves roughly 0.22–0.25 s if cost scales evenly: 1.19 to about 0.94–0.97 s, roughly 20%. Even eliminating all refinement saves only about 0.52 s (44%); this does not justify a new GPU pipeline. Invariant reuse alone has unmeasured upside, no promised under-one-second cold result. **Effort:** 6–12 hours reuse/profiling; 12–24 hours search experiment and corpus validation. **Risk:** accepted recipe, colour safety, review status, undo snapshot, export/Proof behaviour; Still strict applies.

**Memory/VRAM — estimated:** reuse bounded fit proxy statistics, not full frames; existing candidate surfaces recycled, no additional native textures. A new RGB float32 proxy costs 12 bytes/pixel (12 MB at 1 MP) and must fit the existing owner; prefer references to current arrays. **Test plan:** fast suites, especially `test_sdr_match_materialization.py`, then `sdr-match-gpu-interaction.js` and `performance/match-candidate-review.js`; unchanged final certification and export/Proof coverage if recipes differ. **Proposed tolerance:** existing gates/status/highlight order, existing finished-image limits, and no >10% whole-wait regression on fallback cases. **Confidence:** high on cause; medium on reuse, low on safely reducing candidates universally.

## 3. Film Look / lens drags

**Where time goes — measured:** W9 reports stock/grain/print 14.9–16.5 presentations/s and lens 10.5–11.5; device-inclusive frame duration 47–66 ms stock, 76–117 ms lens, warm proxy/mask and encode/submit each under about 1 ms (`performance-table.md:1019-1045`, retained main output `performance/w9-after/film-look.json`). W9 explicitly did not time individual passes. No defensible measured milliseconds per shader can be extracted from these totals; dividing by pass count would be misleading.

**Cause — read in code:** `webgpu-preview.js:6813-6866` encodes response, optional lens extraction + 27 horizontal/vertical pairs + lens combine/scatter, optional halation extract/H/V, finish, scan, structure or response copy. Thus lens bloom alone adds 55 reduced-grid passes before combining; halation adds three. Blur is already separable and downsampled. `graph-scale.js:46-59` selects grid 1 below 2048 long edge, 2 at 2048, 4 at 4096; this intentionally protects small Fit glow from blockiness. `webgpu-shaders.js:2728-2767` converts each of 27 authored component sigmas by lens size, film pixels/mm and spatial scale, with extent capped using 256 times source pixel scale divided by spatial scale. Halation radius uses physical offset and cap 256, edge radius clamped 1–16 (`webgpu-shaders.js:1952-1954`). Radius is recipe/tier-dependent, not one fixed blur radius.

**Proposed fix — estimated:** cache the pre-lens response and the summed unscaled lens-glow surface when only lens strength changes; inspect strength's dependency on scatter/combine separately before declaring the glow independent. Likewise cache pre-grain/print stages for edits downstream of those stages. Files: `webgpu-preview.js` render graph, graph signatures and allocator. Begin with lens strength, the largest measured offender. Invalidation includes stock, physical size, lens filter/size, upstream grade, source/tier/region. Reuse must preserve the original operation order and half-float rounding.

Second option: during drag only, reduce spatial-grid resolution one further step; exact current-policy frame on release/idle, latest-wins generation guarded. This may help stock only when spatial work is active. Reject permanent lower-resolution settle: prior Fit blockiness is documented in `graph-scale.js:46-52`. Reject 'make blur separable' as already done. Pass fusion is a later option: it can change rounding and sampled neighbourhoods, so not a cheap universal win.

**Expected whole-wait gain — estimated:** cached lens strength could remove most 55 glow passes and bring 90 ms toward 40–50 ms (20/s), but total GPU time per stage must be instrumented before committing to this forecast. Spatial-only half-width/height drag work has a fourfold pixel-count reduction, not fourfold whole-frame speedup. Stock at 60 ms needs >10 ms saved; lens at 95 ms needs >45 ms. Settled wait should remain unchanged, not inherit approximate output. **Effort:** 12–24 hours exact lens cache; 8–16 hours preview-only drag tier, including settle tests. **Confidence:** high on graph cause; medium on lens caching; low on per-pass time allocation until timestamped.

**Memory/VRAM — estimated:** two rgba16float retained surfaces cost 16 bytes/pixel: 33.6 MB at 2048x1024 or 59 MB at 2560x1440 before spatial reduction. One additional reduced glow surface costs 8N/grid² bytes. Prefer retaining already allocated graph surfaces and evict under shared adapter budget; never enlarge the 2 GiB policy. Tile keys must not retain an unbounded surface per gesture. **Risk:** stale glow, seams, physical scaling, half-float pinned parity. Exact cache affects a shared shader graph; if shared with delivery invoke Still strict. Drag approximation must be preview-only; export/Proof must never select it.

**Test plan:** fast suites; `performance/film-look-interaction.js` plus `lens-filter-tiled-parity.js`; add film/export/Proof checks covering shared changes (including `film-stock-parity.js`, existing half-precision limitation must not be waived). Instrument GPU timestamp groups where supported: response, 27-pair glow, combine/scatter, halation, finish/scan/structure; otherwise use isolated diagnostic groups, not fabricated individual timings. **Proposed tolerance:** >=20 Fit presentations/s, settled unchanged under existing parity checks, no >10% settle regression. Drag-only image error is informational; exact settle is mandatory. New approximation acceptance should include Steve judging labelled drag/settle transitions, not a newly relaxed delivery tolerance.
