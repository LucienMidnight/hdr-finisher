Geometry

  • Work in whole pixels. Where a control sits inside a bar, the space left over is even, so it splits equally above and below: top bar 44 px with a 34 px plate, panel headers 40 px with 30 px buttons, 5 px each side.
  • Bar dividers are drawn inside the bar (--divider-below) so they take no height.
  • Corners: buttons, tab plates and tooltips --radius-plate (5 px); fields, dropdowns, lists and row highlights --radius-1 (3 px); panels and menus are square.
  • A tab plate keeps --tab-plate-inset (10 px) from the dividers around it.

Icons

  • Tabler outline icons only, from the bundled folder: 24 px grid, 2 px stroke, currentColor. No font glyphs or emoji.
  • 18 px in toolbars and tiles, 16 px in dense rows, --chevron-size for chevrons, --bypass-icon-size for the eye.
  • Idle icons use muted ink and turn to text ink on hover. One symbol means one action.

Sizes

Buttons

  • Every button is a plate: raised face, plate edge and lift. Hover lifts the face to the hover plate; pressed or on is pushed in with accent-strong ink.
  • Tools are icon-only plates, 40 × 34 px, named by a tooltip on hover and keyboard focus: the Local and Retouch tools, Crop and Rotate. No label under the icon.
  • One primary button per area, in the accent plate. Disabled primary falls back to the neutral plate with muted ink at full opacity.
  • Small actions inside headers (preset, bypass) are ghost icons: no plate, muted ink.

Tabs

  • Tabs are one joined plate with a 1 px edge between segments. Each segment carries its own depth: unselected segments are raised with the lift shadow; the selected one is pushed in with only the pressed shadow, so no drop shadow shows under it. Its ink is accent-strong. The same applies to the workflow tabs.
  • Labels are centred. Every segment is as wide as the widest label plus its padding, so no label touches an edge (Luma, Red, Green, Blue are equal).
  • A tab plate sits --tab-plate-inset (10 px) from the dividers above and below it. It never touches a divider.

Fields

  • Fields are recessed wells, the same depth as a pushed button: input surface, plate edge, the pressed inner shadow (--plate-press) and --radius-1 corners. Never square, never a plain outlined box.
  • The label sits above in the small capital field-label style. A unit sits inside the field at the right, in muted ink.
  • Hover shows a grey edge; focus brightens it further. Placeholders use quiet ink.

Text and number fields

Dropdowns

  • A dropdown is a plate with a chevron at the right. It responds to hover like a button: the face lifts and the chevron turns to text ink.

Switches

  • The switch carries its own ON/OFF marks. Do not add "Enabled" or "Disabled" text beside it.
  • A labelled switch is a plain row: no border, no box, no background.
  • A switch that turns a whole section on or off sits in that section's header, at the right.

Sliders, rails and handles

  • Name at the left and value at the right on the first row; the rail takes the whole second row.
  • The rail is a recessed channel. The fill starts at home: neutral at home, accent once changed. A mid-rail home has a 1 px origin line.
  • The handle is a satin bar (--instrument-slider-thumb-w × -h). It does not change size on hover. Drag these to try them.

Panels and sections

  • Every panel starts with a 40 px header: the title in the panel-title style at the left, actions at the right.
  • A section is a titled group inside a panel. Every section ends with a full-width hairline divider, including the last one, so the content never just stops.
  • All section titles use one style (--section-title-font): Afacad medium, sentence case. Sub-sections inside a section use the same style one size down (--subsection-title-font). This replaces the small capitals ("FILE") and the larger title text ("Folders") used today.
  • Small capitals are kept only for field labels.
  • Control Panel modules are disclosure groups: a 34 px header with chevron, name, then preset, Reset with its changed dot, and the eye. An open group shows its body on the raised surface.

Lists and scrollbars

  • A list is one solid surface with thin dividers between rows, no pills. Rows are --list-row-h (34 px).
  • The list's own border clips everything inside, so the scrollbar can't poke out at the corners.
  • The scrollbar rail is always shown, lighter than the list, with a quiet pill thumb that brightens on hover.
  • An empty list keeps the same surface and rail, with one centred muted line. No black blocks.
  • The selected row is neutral: pushed in like a selected tab (--plate-down, --plate-press), with a 2 px accent edge at the left. No coloured background.

Graphs

  • The black plot area is exactly the grid: its edges are the outer grid lines. Nothing black spills past them.
  • Axis labels sit outside the plot on the panel surface, in --font-size-min. Leave enough margin that the first and last labels are never cut off.
  • The home or reference value label uses text ink; other labels use the axis-label ink.
  • A limit or cut-off (the 1000-nit target, the 10K PQ boundary) is a yellow dashed line, --graph-limit-line, dashes 4 × 3 px, with its label in the same yellow. Nothing else in a graph is dashed or yellow, apart from selected control points.
  • Zero and identity lines are neutral. Curves are neutral too unless the graph's channel gives them a colour (Red, Green, Blue).
  • Each graph keeps its own curve, node and band tokens (listed in the guideline). Only the frame is shared. Selected control points are yellow in every graph; the Exposure Bands influence band is orange.

Waiting for approval

These are new tokens or changes. Nothing here goes into the app until Steve approves it.

  • Section titles: --section-title-font (Afacad medium, --font-size-lg, sentence case) and --subsection-title-font (same, --font-size-md). Replaces small capitals in Details and the larger titles in Pinned.
  • Default Dark depth (switch at the top left, on by default): app #050505, panel #080808, raised #101010, input #0a0a0a (darker than Modern Dark's #0c0c0c / #121212 / #191919, never pure black). Raised plates run #383838 to #1c1c1c with a black edge, a bright top line, a strong drop shadow and a faint light lip; pushed plates are a dark well with a heavy top inner shadow. The viewer and graph plots stay pure black. This brings Default Dark's buttons close to Modern Dark's, on a darker background with Default Dark's stronger lines and text.
  • Neutral selection: --accent-bed becomes neutral in every theme: the pressed plate, --plate-down, instead of a tint of the accent colour (shown on this page, including its swatch). Selected rows, cells and pressed filter buttons are pushed in; selection keeps only its accent edge or accent text. It's used in about 25 places today (Library rows and cells, tags, filters, Export presets and photos, HSL picker).
  • Exposure Bands influence band: orange instead of yellow, --equalizer-influence-wash: #ff7a1f1f (the existing band orange #ff7a1f at about 12%; today #efbb55 at 9%). Selected control points in every graph stay yellow.
  • Graph marks: a new token --graph-limit-line = --attention (yellow) for every limit or cut-off, dashed 4 × 3 px, label in the same yellow. Today the Exposure Bands 10K line uses a muted gold (--equalizer-pq) with a yellow label, and Highlight Compression draws no target line. --equalizer-zero becomes the neutral strong grid grey instead of purple. Highlight Compression's curve is typed in as cyan (#68d7ed) in highlight-controls.js; it should use the neutral curve token, and its dashed grey no-change line becomes a solid faint identity line so only limits are dashed.
  • Lists: --list-row-h 34 px and --scrollbar-w 10 px.
  • Existing exceptions, kept as they are: the primary button, the changed slider fill and the On switch knob mix the accent or white with other colours. The histogram fill uses 55% opacity. These were in the app before the tokens rule. Keep them, or replace them with new tokens?
