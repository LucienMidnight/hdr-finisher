How this works

Global works like DxO: All plus eight bands, each with its own edit. Use the picker to move or widen a band, or to add and exclude colours. Picking while All is selected (or pressing +) makes a Custom channel from any mix of colours.

Local grade has one selection, which can hold several colours, and one edit, applied inside the demo ellipse. Alt-click the photo to move the ellipse. The dots are quick starts.

Each colour range has a point on the wheel: click it to edit, drag to move, right-click (or Delete) to remove. Excluded ranges sit inside the ring. Double-click a slider to reset it. Wheel handles take arrow keys (Shift for bigger steps). The preview is an SDR browser decode, limited to 1000 pixels on the long edge.
